The actual difference

A raffle ends the moment it starts.
This runs for a week.

A typical raffle

Buy a number, wait for a draw

  • One transaction, no reason to return
  • Outcome is pure chance
  • Nothing to check on day 2, 3, or 4
  • Kids can't really take part in the decision
An Ecoracing school race

Build a balloon, follow it live

  • Buyers choose how their balloon is built
  • Real weather data moves the race daily
  • A live leaderboard to check in on all week
  • Kids can pick the build with their family
The mechanic

Every balloon is a small decision, not a lucky dip

Buyers set the build

Before the race starts, whoever buys a balloon chooses how it's built. Each choice trades off differently once real weather hits the course.

Why it raises more

You're not limited to who walks past the school gate

Every balloon has its own link

A bag pack or cake sale only reaches people who physically turn up. A balloon race doesn't have that ceiling — every entry gets a shareable link a family can send to anyone, anywhere.

  • → Send it to grandparents, aunties, family friends — anyone who'd normally miss the school-gate ask
  • → Share it in a work Slack or WhatsApp group — colleagues don't need to be near the school to buy in
  • → Name the balloon after your child or class, so whoever you send it to knows exactly who they're backing
